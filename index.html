Summary

Doctoral-trained AI-in-education researcher and program builder. I have designed a school-university-industry AI partnership program, a pedagogical model for teaching AI and machine learning, and an implementation framework schools can adopt. I combine rigorous qualitative research, hands-on technical teaching (Python, TensorFlow, robotics) and a chemistry and biology background with university teaching, curriculum design and stakeholder leadership.

Selected Achievements

  • Formed BALRS (Bay Area AI LEGO Robot Specialists), an international network of AI and robotics researchers and teachers: BALRS on LinkedIn
  • Designed the "AI Our Way" school-university-industry partnership program, the McGregor Model of AI-Ed Pedagogy, and AI Education Curriculum: School Implementation Framework, all from a four-cycle action research study.
  • Founded the MW-AI Robotics Club: within one year, 15 students with no prior programming experience competed in their first FIRST LEGO League tournament.
  • Member of the CSIRO-funded TRAIN (Teachers' Rights and AI Network) research team; completed CSIRO's ON Prime 14 program (2023).
  • Critical friend and advisor for design and development of Victoria University Generative AI Learning Module for Undergraduate and Postgraduate courses.
  • Chair of the Early Childhood Development section, Action Group for Equality, Inclusion, Gender and Diversity in Research (AGEIGD) committee.
  • Co-Secretary of a higher-degree research student conference committee.
  • Sub-editor and chapter author, Doing Doctoral Research: Narratives of Practice (Brill | Sense).
